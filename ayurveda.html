Ayurveda

A 5,000-year-old system of natural healing, preserved through education, culture, and artificial intelligence.

The Origin of Ayurveda

Ayurveda originated in ancient India and is one of the world's oldest holistic healthcare systems. Its principles are documented in texts such as the Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita.

The Three Doshas

Vata

Air and Space. Governs movement and creativity.

Pitta

Fire and Water. Governs digestion and metabolism.

Kapha

Earth and Water. Governs stability and strength.

Medicinal Plants

Tulsi

Ashwagandha

Used for stress management and vitality.

Neem

Traditional plant for skin and oral health.

Aloe Vera

Known for soothing and healing properties.

Turmeric

Ancient anti-inflammatory golden spice.
