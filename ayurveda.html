Ayurveda

A 5,000-year-old system of natural science evolving from ancient wisdom to modern AI-driven understanding.

Origin of Ayurveda

Ayurveda originated in ancient India as one of the world’s earliest holistic healthcare systems. It is based on balancing body, mind, and environment through natural principles.

Core Philosophy

Balance

Health is defined as harmony between body, mind, and consciousness.

Five Elements

Everything in nature is composed of Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space.

Dosha System

Vata, Pitta, and Kapha govern physical and mental functions.

Evolution of Ayurveda

Vedic Era

Ayurveda begins in ancient scriptures like the Vedas and Upanishads.

Classical Period

Textual codification in Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita.

Medieval Era

Integration with regional healing systems across Asia.

Modern Era

Scientific validation and integration with modern healthcare.

Ayurveda in the Modern World

Today, Ayurveda is being studied alongside biology, pharmacology, and preventive medicine. Its principles are influencing wellness, lifestyle science, and digital health systems.

Future of Ayurveda with AI

With AI systems like KORA, Ayurveda is transforming into an intelligent knowledge system — combining ancient wisdom with data-driven personalization and global accessibility.

Medicinal Plants of Ayurveda

Tulsi

Known as Holy Basil, widely used in traditional wellness systems.

Ashwagandha

Traditionally associated with strength and stress resilience.

Neem

Historically used for purification and skin-related applications.

Aloe Vera

Known for its cooling and restorative properties in traditional systems.

Turmeric

A key ancient spice studied for its natural bioactive compounds.
